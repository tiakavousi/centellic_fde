* Hybrid search = Lexical + semantic search
* Use lexical search for account numbers (need an exact match), semantic search for descriptions (wording/phrasing can vary, but meaning is consistent)
* Lexical Search = Doing a literal keyword scan. Your standard ctrl + F. Exact match. Can't find different word that means the same thing.
  * Limited if we do not have the exact information.
* Semantic Search = Looking for which documents are most likely to contain what king of information. Can piece together the meaning of words/phrases.
* Emebeddings don't capture polarity well, only meaninig
